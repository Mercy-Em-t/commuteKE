This Proprietary Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective as of **[Date]** by and between **[Your Company Name]** (the "Company") and **[Contractor/Employee Name]** (the "Creator").

**1. Purpose**
As a condition of the Creator's engagement with the Company, the Creator will have access to the Company's Proprietary Information and may develop Inventions for the Company. This Agreement ensures the Company owns all such Inventions.

**2. Proprietary Information**
"Proprietary Information" refers to any trade secrets, confidential knowledge, data, or any other proprietary information of the Company. The Creator agrees to hold all Proprietary Information in strict confidence and not to disclose it to any third party or use it for any purpose other than the performance of duties for the Company.

**3. Assignment of Inventions**
The Creator agrees that all right, title, and interest in and to any and all inventions, original works of authorship, developments, concepts, improvements, designs, discoveries, ideas, trademarks, or trade secrets, whether or not patentable or registrable under copyright or similar laws, which the Creator may solely or jointly conceive, develop, or reduce to practice during the period of time the Creator is engaged by the Company (collectively, "Inventions"), are the sole property of the Company. 

The Creator hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to all such Inventions, including all related intellectual property rights.

**4. Maintenance of Records**
The Creator agrees to keep and maintain adequate and current written records of all Inventions made during the term of engagement. These records will be available to and remain the sole property of the Company.

**5. Patent and Copyright Registrations**
The Creator agrees to assist the Company, or its designee, at the Company's expense, in every proper way to secure the Company's rights in the Inventions and any copyrights, patents, mask work rights, or other intellectual property rights relating thereto in any and all countries.
